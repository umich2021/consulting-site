## What I do

01. Your own branded app
An iPhone and Android app with your name and logo on the customer's home screen, published under your own App Store and Google Play accounts. I handle the store submissions and reviews.

02. Ordering, rewards and booking
Customers browse your menu or services, order ahead or book a time, and earn rewards for coming back. The app is designed around how your customers already buy from you.

03. Connected to the systems you use
The app syncs with the point-of-sale, inventory or booking software you already pay for, so your staff keep one system and the menu in the app stays current.

04. Push notifications and upkeep
Send offers on slow days and reminders when a customer is due back. I keep the app updated for new phone OS releases and store policy changes.

Regulated industries: I also build for regulated industries, where Apple and Google add their own rules on top of the state's. A cannabis dispensary app, for example, has to verify age, show the license number and follow state rules on payments and promotions. I build that in from the start.

## How a project runs

1. A short call. Twenty minutes on how customers find you, buy from you and come back. If an app won't help, I'll tell you.
2. A demo on your phone. I come back with a working app under your name, installed on your phone, so you're judging something real.
3. A paid pilot. Four to six weeks live with your customers at a reduced price, with one agreed number to move, such as repeat orders, measured against where you started.
4. Ongoing support. A flat monthly fee covers hosting, app updates and changes as your business or the rules change.
