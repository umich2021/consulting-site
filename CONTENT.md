## Home: what we do

01. Your own branded app
An iPhone, Android or web app with your name and logo on it. Phone apps go out under your own App Store and Google Play accounts, and web apps run on your own domain. We handle the store submissions and reviews.

02. Ordering, rewards and booking
Customers browse your menu or services, order ahead or book a time, and earn rewards for coming back. We design it around how your customers already buy from you.

03. Connected to the systems you use
The app syncs with the point-of-sale, inventory or booking software you already pay for, so your staff keep one system and your menu stays current on every screen.

04. Notifications and upkeep
Send offers on slow days and reminders when a customer is due back. We keep your apps updated for new phone and browser releases and for app store policy changes.

Regulated industries: We also build for regulated industries, where Apple and Google add their own rules on top of the state's. A cannabis dispensary app, for example, has to verify age, show the license number and follow state rules on payments and promotions. We build that in from the start.

## Work

Screenshots in `public/images/work/`. The Nexus screenshot comes from endaltechnologies.com.

01. Snag (links to snaganything.com)
Marketplace · Live
Shoppers name their price for a pair of running shoes, and every store on Snag with their size can accept or pass. Brands set a minimum advertised price, but a store can still make a private deal, so the shopper often pays less than the store's own site charges. The store gets a buyer who already knows their size.

02. Nexus
AI education · Divit Endal at Endal Technologies, with UNC's Shuford Entrepreneurship Program
College athletes with name, image and likeness (NIL) deals get paid like small businesses but get generic financial advice. Nexus builds each athlete a course around their own deals, and its AI tutor answers questions by citing the lesson it used. Tax figures come from calculators instead of the model, and anything that reads like personal financial advice goes to an educator.

03. Aventos Analytics
Brand visibility in AI answers · Live
When people ask ChatGPT or Google's AI about a product, the answer shapes what they buy. Aventos Analytics turns a brand's Google Ads keywords into hundreds of prompts, runs them across AI models and records every mention, claim and source. Brands see their share of voice against competitors and which sources drive it.

## Process

1. A short call. Twenty minutes on how customers find you, buy from you and come back. If an app won't help, we'll tell you.
2. A demo on your phone. We come back with a working app under your name, on your phone, so you're judging something real.
3. A paid pilot. Four to six weeks live with your customers at a reduced price, with one agreed number to move, such as repeat orders, measured against where you started.
4. Ongoing support. A flat monthly fee covers hosting, app updates and changes as your business or the rules change.
